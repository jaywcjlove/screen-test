Feedback & Support  
===  

`Screen Test` is a professional macOS application designed to help you quickly and easily evaluate your display's image quality and visual performance. It includes 25+ test functions to detect dead pixels, backlight bleeding, vertical banding, screen uniformity issues, and more. Suitable for screen testing, display detection, monitor testing, and dead pixel detection scenarios, it's a practical tool for display assessment.

Your feedback and support are invaluable! If you encounter any issues, have questions, or would like to suggest new features, please feel free to reach out. Your input helps us continuously improve `Screen Test` and ensures we deliver the best possible user experience.  

You can contact us through the in-app feedback feature or by sending an email to **kennyiseeyou@gmail.com**. We are dedicated to providing timely support and ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for you. Thank you for choosing Screen Test!
